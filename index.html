A series on diffusion MRI

Diffusion Without Equations

Diffusion MRI is one of the most widely used techniques in MRI, and one of the hardest to explain without a whiteboard full of math. This series takes a different route. It traces the history of the field back to Einstein, uses an interactive animation of track runners to show how a diffusion sequence works, and reports what the diffusion community thinks comes next.

When you are ready for the equations, the final post collects six lectures from ISMRM educational sessions into a free course, with a quiz question for each key topic.
