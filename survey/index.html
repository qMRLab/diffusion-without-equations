The community survey

Originally published July 28, 2017 on ISMRM's MR Pulse blog

To close the series, we asked the diffusion MRI community where the field stands and where it is headed. The survey is now closed, and 155 people responded.

The results are in! See what people had to say in Part 3.

These were the questions:

  1. What is your background?

    • Biology
    • Computer Science (image processing, artificial intelligence, statistics)
    • Biomedical Engineering
    • Medicine
    • Physics
    • Other (please specify)

  2. Has diffusion MRI peaked?

    • Diffusion MRI has already peaked a while ago and I'm not sure why people are still working on it!
    • Diffusion MRI is currently at its peak!
    • The future is promising – diffusion MRI has not reached its peak yet!

  3. What do you think is the biggest problem in diffusion MRI research?

    • Insufficient understanding of biophysical origins of the diffusion signal
    • Reproducibility between vendors/platforms
    • Disconnect between MRI researchers and life sciences
    • Disconnect between MRI researchers and clinicians
    • Other (please specify)

  4. What do you think should be emphasized more in research methodology?

    • Building phantoms and performing calibration
    • Building adequate tissue models
    • Constructing platforms for sharing data and analysis tools
    • Validation in animal models
    • Pilot studies for clinical translation
    • Other (please specify)

  5. What do you think is the biggest problem for quantitative diffusion in the clinic?

    • Too much pre-processing
    • Long acquisition times
    • Reliability in results
    • Confusion in interpretation
    • Reliability in tracking algorithms
    • Other (please specify)

  6. Tell us more! What attracts you to the field of diffusion MRI? Why do you think diffusion MRI is so popular, in comparison to other MRI modalities? Where do you think diffusion MRI will be in 10 years? Please feel free to leave your name if you wish.
